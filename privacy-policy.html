Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how EarnGig, operated by AHRUH ENTERPRISES, handles information provided by users of the platform.

1. About EarnGig

EarnGig is an online earning platform operated by AHRUH ENTERPRISES, a CAC-registered business based in Lagos, Nigeria.

EarnGig provides members with access to eligible activities including tasks, reading activities, content creation, referrals and other platform opportunities.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use EarnGig, we may collect information necessary to create and operate your account.

Account information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Information associated with your EarnGig account

Payment and withdrawal information

Where necessary to process withdrawals or other platform-related transactions, EarnGig may require relevant bank account information.

Task and activity information

When you participate in activities, we may receive information you submit as part of those activities, including screenshots, files, task responses or other proof required for review.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Request

EarnGig does not require users to provide a date of birth or profile photograph as part of the information currently collected for ordinary account use.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide access to platform activities.
  • Review task submissions and proof.
  • Process eligible rewards and withdrawals.
  • Communicate with users about their accounts.
  • Investigate complaints, disputes and payment issues.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the platform.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud or unauthorized activity.

5. Task Submissions

Users may be required to submit screenshots, files, responses or other evidence when completing certain activities.

Such information may be reviewed to determine whether the applicable activity requirements have been satisfied.

6. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through EarnGig and to prevent unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

Users should also protect their account credentials and should not share passwords or authentication information with other people.

7. Information Sharing

We do not treat your personal information as public information simply because you use EarnGig.

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to operate the platform, process a requested transaction, investigate a dispute, protect the platform or comply with a lawful requirement.

8. Third-Party Services

EarnGig may rely on third-party technology and service providers to operate parts of the platform, including hosting, authentication, databases, communications or payment-related services.

Information handled by such services may be subject to their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

9. Your Responsibilities

Users are responsible for providing accurate information and keeping their account information up to date.

Users should contact EarnGig promptly if they believe their account has been accessed without authorization.

10. Privacy Questions and Complaints

If you have a question, complaint or concern about how your information is handled, contact us by email.
