Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests and payment-related complaints are handled by EarnGig, operated by AHRUH ENTERPRISES.

1. About This Policy

EarnGig is operated by AHRUH ENTERPRISES, a CAC-registered business based in Lagos, Nigeria.

This policy explains the process for reporting a payment issue or requesting a refund where a refund may be applicable.

2. How to Request a Refund

Refund requests must be submitted by email.

To request a refund, contact:

ahruhenterprises@gmail.com

You may also contact:

earngigofficial@gmail.com

3. Information to Include

When requesting a refund or reporting a payment issue, please provide enough information for the transaction to be identified and investigated.

This may include:

  • Your full name.
  • The email address associated with your EarnGig account.
  • A clear description of the issue.
  • Relevant transaction details.
  • Relevant transaction history or payment records.
  • Any other information reasonably necessary to investigate the issue.

4. Review of Refund Requests

Refund requests are reviewed based on the circumstances of the transaction and the information provided by the requester.

Additional information may be requested where necessary to investigate the issue.

A refund is not automatically guaranteed simply because a request has been submitted. The circumstances of the transaction will be reviewed before a decision is made.

5. Payment and Transaction Issues

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, experienced a payment problem or encountered another transaction issue, contact support as soon as possible.

Please include the relevant transaction information so the matter can be investigated efficiently.

6. Account and Activity Issues

Where a payment or account issue is connected to a particular EarnGig activity, task or transaction, additional activity information may be reviewed.

This may include the applicable activity, transaction history, account records or submitted proof.

7. Withdrawals

Eligible users may request withdrawals from their available balance at any time.

A withdrawal request is different from a refund request. Withdrawal issues should also be reported by email with the relevant transaction or account information.

8. Support and Disputes

For complaints, disputes, payment issues or refund questions, contact:

AHRUH ENTERPRISES
Lagos, Nigeria

ahruhenterprises@gmail.com

earngigofficial@gmail.com

9. Policy Updates

This Refund Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to EarnGig or its refund and payment procedures.

The latest version published on this page will apply from its stated effective date.
